Return policy

30 days. Unused. Easy refunds.

If an item is not right, send it back within 30 days of delivery. We refund the original payment through Stripe once the return arrives.

What can be returned

Items in original condition, unworn or unused, with tags and packaging. Accessories that shipped with the product should come back with it.

What cannot be returned

Opened personal-care items, gift cards, and products damaged by misuse. Final-sale pieces are marked on the product page before you pay.

How to start a return

  1. Open the contact form and include your order ID.
  2. We reply with a prepaid return label within one business day.
  3. Drop the package at any carrier location. Keep the receipt until your refund posts.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days after we inspect the return. Shipping charged at checkout is refunded when the return is our error or a defective item. Return shipping is free on standard orders.

Exchanges

We do not hold inventory for one-to-one exchanges. Return the original item, then place a new order for the piece you want.

Need a label?

We will send one.

Include the Stripe checkout email and order ID so we can match your payment.
