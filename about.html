Our story

A small studio for everyday objects that last.

Studio Shop started as a workbench project: a handful of well-made pieces we wanted on our own desks, wrists, and weekend trips. We still keep the catalog short on purpose. Every item is chosen for materials, comfort, and the way it looks after years of use.

We pack orders ourselves, ship from the United States, and use Stripe so checkout stays encrypted. If something is not right, our return policy is written to make it easy to send it back.

What we stand for

  • Curated, not crowded

    A few products at a time, with details we can actually stand behind.

  • Fair checkout

    Clear pricing, 8% tax at checkout, and free standard shipping on every order.

  • Human support

    Questions go to a real person. Reach us from the contact page, not a ticket maze.
